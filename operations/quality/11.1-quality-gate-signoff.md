## Security Notes

- `GET /v1/connectors/token` is guarded by `session.scope === 'internal'` — customer sessions cannot retrieve raw OAuth credentials.
- `DELETE /v1/episodic-memory/:id` filters by `tenantId` from the session — cross-tenant deletion attempts return 404 (not 403) to avoid tenant enumeration.
- `_connector_tokens` injection in runtime-server.ts is env-gated — no `RUNTIME_INTERNAL_SESSION_TOKEN` means no credential fetch attempt.
